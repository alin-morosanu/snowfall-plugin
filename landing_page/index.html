Get Started in 60 Seconds

Copy and paste - that's it!

Step 1: Add the files

<script src="snowfall-plugin.js" data-auto-init></script>

Step 2: Customize (Optional)

SnowfallPlugin.init({
  numberOfSnowflakes: 50,
  smallSnowflakeDensity: 2,
  numberOfLayers: 2
});
